I Cannot Change the World

The world began from nothing. I think of that often. A collection of gas and asteroid collisions resulting in a giant rock in the middle of space, eventually filled with water and then a billion micro-organisms that somehow led to the animals we see today.
I lay in my room one night, Fairy lights twinkling around the edges of the ceiling while ambient synths play through the headphones on my ears. Worrying about how my life will play out. I was roughly seventeen, debating the worth of college and the consequences of capitalism on my work life and my likelihood of escaping it all. Anticipating the arrival of World War 3 as the fear mongers of Instagram fulminated about its beginning.
Nobody is changing the world so I must. I must be the one to help people, I must take a position of power to do so. But I doubt I could be capable of that. I will make movies with political parallels so that people understand, maybe that will do something. I am an artist. I must make art. Maybe that will do something.
I cannot change the world.
“Dead People’s Things” Plays in my ear as I contemplate this. I cannot possibly save everyone. We are all going to die.
What is the point then? If I cannot change the world? If I do not change the world my existence is all for not. If I do not make a difference, then did I really ever matter? The stories I made up, the worlds I created, the clothes I want to make, the portraits I’ve drawn, they won’t mean anything to anybody, not as much as they do to me. What is the point then, if I cannot change the world?
Many people contemplate this, I think that is what religion is for. How different it must be, to believe you will live forever when you’re done living here. With the people you love, in eternal happiness. How different it must be, to devote your entire self to something you don’t truly know exists, except in your heart, or your soul, that’s what I’ve been told. “God has a plan,” that is comforting I must admit. To believe we all serve a greater purpose, that we mean something to some greater power.
We do not mean anything.
There is no reason for us to be here, other than the fact that we are. Human ancestors were some of the smartest, so they survived. Drove others out, built their homes on land claimed to themselves. We multiplied, and multiplied, and multiplied. We always take more. Some believe this is what makes humanity special, because other animals could never accomplish what we have. That is why we don’t consider ourselves animals. Because we are above Mother Nature’s game of survival, we must have superseded her. But we are still playing the game.
Humanity is inherently selfish. We make as much as we can to live a life. Then we hoard, and we hoard. That’s what animals do, they hoard as much as they can. It’s an instinct, for when something goes wrong. We so desperately hold on to the thing that will save us. It used to be food. Now it is money. That is why I must save the world, change it for the better so that we stop living in our unrecognized dystopia. But to save the world is against animal nature, and unfortunately, I am only human. That is a relief really, a load off my shoulders.
I cannot change the world.
What is the point then, If I am nothing in the context of the earth, of the universe? I guess there is no point. Isn’t that wonderful though? That we have no purpose? I guess that means I get to make my own. Maybe I cannot change the world, but I can change the lives of those closest to me. I can make their lives better, more enjoyable. I can live life the way I want to live it. Because…
I cannot change the world.
But the world began from nothing, I think of that often. And the earth didn’t stop spinning when the dinosaurs went extinct, it began again. We think we are so unnatural with our mechanics and giant factories, but we are just doing what any animal does. We survive. An invasive species that got out of hand a long time ago. We are the next extinction event, yet we are desperately holding onto a world that is destined to collapse, as if it is the end.
Nobody can save the world.
And it is the end.
But only for us.
